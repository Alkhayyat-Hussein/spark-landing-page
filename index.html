Story Presentation Analytics & Reporting Kit

Capture real outcomes fast, link them to KPIs, and deliver donor-ready stories that build trust.

Elevator Pitch

That is why we built SPARK. SPARK is a web-based impact reporting toolkit that makes it easy to capture, organize, and communicate impact in one place. It combines clear KPIs with real stories and media, allowing organizations to present both the data and the human impact, without adding extra workload.

Instead of spending hours piecing together reports, staff can quickly generate clear, credible impact summaries that donors understand and trust.

Toolkit Flow

Capture

Log stories, photos, and outcomes in one quick, mobile-first intake.

Organize

Automatically connect stories to KPIs so impact is searchable and consistent.

Showcase

Generate donor-ready summaries with data and human moments side by side.

Why It Matters

More funding

Donors see proof fast, boosting trust and repeat support.

Less admin

Teams spend minutes, not hours, building reports.

Stronger stories

Every success story becomes visible, measurable, and shareable.
